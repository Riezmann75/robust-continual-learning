# Third-party code and attribution

reproduce/augmix_operations.py contains Google LLC's AugMix PIL operations.
Its copyright header is retained. The full Apache License 2.0 is preserved in
reproduce/AUGMIX_LICENSE.

Source: https://github.com/google-research/augmix

The experimental runners were developed in this project. MetaMix is a
paper-guided first-order implementation, not verified author code.
No project-wide license grant has been selected; the bundled AugMix license
applies to that third-party component.

CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-10-C datasets are downloaded separately and are not included.
